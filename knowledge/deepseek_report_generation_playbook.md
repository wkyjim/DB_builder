# DeepSeek Report Generation Playbook

The report must be analytical, not summary-only.

For every major section, include:

1. What the data says
2. What it means
3. Why it matters
4. What could be wrong
5. Portfolio implication

Required report structure:

# DeepSeek CIO House View

## Executive Summary

* Dominant regime
* Confidence
* Market character
* Portfolio stance
* 3 most important conclusions

## Market Bullishness / Bearishness

* Bullish factors
* Bearish factors
* Neutral factors
* Bullish / Neutral / Bearish probability split
* Critical interpretation

## Investor Sentiment

* Institutional sentiment
* Retail/speculative sentiment
* volatility signal
* risk appetite signal
* conclusion

## Cross-Asset Confirmation

* equities
* rates
* FX / dollar
* commodities
* volatility
* crypto
* conclusion

## Sector Strength Ranking

For each sector:

* tactical regime
* secular support
* key opportunity
* key risk
* portfolio bias

## Highest Conviction Opportunities

Tier 1:

* strongest tactical and secular alignment

Tier 2:

* good opportunities but with tactical or macro caveats

Tier 3:

* long-term themes in tactical correction

## Tactical Positioning

* overweight
* neutral
* underweight
* avoid/reduce

## Tactical Buy List

* buy on pullbacks
* hold / monitor
* avoid chasing

## Tactical Sell / Reduce List

* reduce into strength
* avoid
* hedges

## Key Risks

List top risks and explain portfolio impact.

## Risk Management

* equity exposure range
* cash range
* hedge ideas
* max aggression rule
* what not to do

## Alternative Scenario

What would make current view wrong?

## Final House View

One concise CIO paragraph.
