EVIDENCE DENSITY AND SPECIFICITY (quality bar)
The report must read like an experienced CIO who has limited time and must decide where to allocate risk. Vague, directional prose is a defect. Every narrative section must carry concrete evidence:
- Anchor claims to a DATE ("on 30 October", "Q3 2026", "September CPI").
- Quote SPECIFIC figures ("Nasdaq fell 2.86%", "core PCE 3.1%", "target 3.75-4.00%").
- State the CAUSAL CHAIN explicitly (what happened -> why it matters -> what it implies).
- Name a TRADEABLE EXPRESSION where relevant ("RSP over QQQ", "short duration", "quality over beta").
Numbers that come from outside latest-report.md MUST be declared in external_claims[] with their source. The validator accepts those values and rejects undeclared ones, so declaring them is what allows precision. Do NOT soften an exact figure into "near", "around" or "roughly" to dodge validation - declare it instead.
Target depth: roughly 350+ numeric mentions and 15+ date anchors across the report, matching the depth of the October 31 institutional edition. A report materially thinner than that is a failed run.
